María Dina is 66 years old. She has high cholesterol and high blood pressure, and cataracts have left her partially blind, which affects her quality of life a great deal.

She has had problems with her teeth for years and lost most of them at a young age. Her four children help her cover her basic needs, but she could not meet the cost of restoring her oral health.

Dina had extractions and periodontal preparation, followed by several months of healing. She can now show her new smile, paid for with bitcoin.
