Maximilian is 68. He has arterial hypertension, diabetes and pain through his body from arthritis, none of which keeps him from the farming work he has done all his life.

At the dental consultation we found abundant bacterial plaque, gingivitis and bleeding, some fractured teeth and others necrotic, or dead, leaving root remnants. We also found that the denture he had been wearing for more than twenty years was broken and in very poor condition. On his income he had never been able even to attempt a dental appointment.
