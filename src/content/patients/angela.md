Angela is 73. She had worn a denture for more than 25 years. About a decade ago she fractured it in a fall, and for years after that she lived without a working prosthesis.

At the clinic the team found severe bone loss from the long absence of her denture.

She has recovered her prosthesis and her smile, thanks to Bitcoin. "My long wait is over!" she said.
