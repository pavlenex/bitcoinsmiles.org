Dominga is 67 years old. She has spent most of her life on housework, working in the fields and helping her husband, who is a fisherman.

She also helps care for her 13-year-old granddaughter, who recently had a stroke. Her own health has come second. She has been waiting months for surgery on a herniated disc that makes walking difficult.

She had not seen a dentist in 35 years. Her denture was broken and in poor condition, and the teeth she had left were badly worn and almost gone.

Thanks to Bitcoin Smiles, she received the dental care she needed, and can smile with confidence again.
