Reyna is the mother of five and looks after her husband and her home. She has had hypertension for years, and she lost all her teeth 35 years ago.

"It wasn't easy getting used to life without teeth," she says.

The loss brought on digestive problems and gastric reflux as well. She could not afford to replace a poorly made prosthesis that was breaking and irritating the lining of her mouth.

In her spare time Reyna looks after her grandchildren, and her own children help her out. Her smile is back.
