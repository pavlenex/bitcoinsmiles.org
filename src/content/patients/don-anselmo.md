Don Anselmo is 63 and has spent his whole life working in agriculture. He has arthritis, which causes him severe pain in his back, shoulders and knees.

He lost his teeth years ago to widespread chronic periodontitis with severe bone loss. “My teeth just fell out on their own,” he says. His upper dentures had been fractured for years, which caused him further occlusal problems when chewing.

Bitcoin Smiles has fitted him with a full set of dentures. His occlusion and his oral health have improved, and so has his confidence.
