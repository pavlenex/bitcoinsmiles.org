Maria is 66 years old and lives on very little. She keeps house and makes tortillas, and above all she is the full-time carer for her sister Lucila, 56, who has epilepsy.

She has high blood pressure, diabetes, kidney problems and incontinence. Maria lost her permanent teeth early in life, and at the dental consultation we found fractured and infected teeth. Missing teeth and the lack of a proper denture had made chewing difficult, and that is no longer the case, thanks to funding in bitcoin.
