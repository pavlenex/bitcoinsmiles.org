Rosaura is 42 and lives on very limited means. She gets by looking after cattle and washing other people's clothes in the river.

She is raising three children, one of whom has an intellectual disability. She told us she had been physically and psychologically abused in the past.

Although she is still young, she arrived at the consultation with what was left of her decaying teeth, chronic bleeding in the gums, severe tooth mobility and loss of bone in the jaw.

She had fourteen extractions and spent almost four months recovering. She wept with joy when she received her new dentures, thanks to Bitcoin.
