Margarita is a housewife. She had five children, and like her husband several of them work in construction as bricklayers. She has seven grandchildren, two of whom are in her care.

Margarita lost her permanent teeth early, and four years ago an infection took the last of them. She does not have the means to pay for private dental care and get her smile back.

The surprise of her life is that bitcoin is what brought her a smile.
