Misael is a 60-year-old farmer. He has vision problems and hypertension. In his youth he lost some of his permanent teeth to decay.

He came to the clinic with a fixed partial denture in bad condition, broken, that had damaged the teeth he had left. His finances had kept him from dental care for decades.

With his new smile, Misael has his confidence and optimism back.
