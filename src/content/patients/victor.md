Almost three years ago Victor and his wife were in a serious accident in which several people died. He was left in intensive care with multiple fractures and other injuries, and doctors gave him little chance of recovery and thought he would never walk again. He lost almost all of his teeth.

Procedure. Hygienic phase: periodontal condition and 13 preliminary extractions. Rehabilitation phase: complete flexibility.

Victor has since made a full recovery, and he has his smile back.
